# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One user: the developer. Context: opening the app mid-workout or right after, often in a gym environment with low ambient light, sweaty hands, and a short attention window. The goal is to log and close in under 30 seconds. Occasional deeper sessions reviewing analytics or adjusting nutrition targets, but those are secondary.

## Product Purpose

Frictionless is a personal workout tracker with AI-powered text parsing. The user types a free-form description of their workout in Thai or English, the AI structures it, and they confirm before saving. It also tracks nutrition, bodyweight, rest days, and progress analytics. Success means: log feels effortless, data is always there when wanted, zero friction between intention and record.

## Brand Personality

Precision tool. Clinical, data-first, no decoration that doesn't carry information. Like a finely tuned instrument — quiet confidence, tight execution, no whimsy. Three words: precise, quiet, capable.

## Anti-references

- **MyFitnessPal / generic fitness apps**: cluttered calorie-counter energy, aggressive upselling, garish contrast, data buried under chrome
- **Strava / social fitness**: gamification, social validation, orange urgency, community noise
- **SaaS dashboard clichés**: big-number hero metrics, gradient text, identical card grids, progress rings for their own sake
- **Neon cyberpunk**: heavy glows, neon on black, over-the-top visual intensity that competes with the data

## Design Principles

1. **Log speed above all.** The primary path (text input → AI parse → confirm → save) should feel instant. No extra taps, no confirmation dialogs for obvious actions, no animation that adds latency to perception.
2. **Data is the decoration.** Numbers, charts, and structure carry the visual weight. Surfaces that don't hold data should recede. Chrome and ornament are earned, not assumed.
3. **Earn the dark.** The dark theme exists because this is used in a gym under low ambient light after physical exertion — not as an aesthetic flex. Every dark surface should serve legibility and reduce visual strain.
4. **Precision over personality.** Tight spacing, clean edges, consistent rhythm. Avoid rounding, softening, or warming things up for the sake of "friendliness." The interface should feel like equipment.
5. **Trust the user.** Single user, high familiarity. No tooltips for obvious actions, no hand-holding copy, no onboarding flows. The UI can be terse and expert-facing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single personal-use application, no public accessibility requirements. However: high contrast between text and surface should be maintained for low-light gym environments. Minimum 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large UI elements. Tap targets ≥ 44px (used during physical activity, possible gloves or fatigue).
